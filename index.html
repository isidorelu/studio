Statement

ISIDORELU.COM is a creative and production studio in New York, founded by the musician and designer Isid Or. It develops and produces original music, film, photography, live events, websites, and merchandise, and takes on audio and creative commissions for clients.

The studio grew out of @isidcore, an Instagram blog and Substack by Isid Or covering pop culture, fashion, and the arts. In the summer of 2026, it produced the self-financed campaign for the Japanese mini-album Sincerely, Mr. Love & Pop, including a [1] full-production music video, three [3] merchandise lines, and two [2] release parties in New York. The same summer, the studio took on its first corporate client, scoring five [5] immersive activations for Kodansha House in Los Angeles and New York.

In November 2026, the creative studio, production company, and blog consolidated under ISIDORELU.COM. The studio is open to commissions.

KODANSHA HOUSE — LOS ANGELES & NEW YORK

Client work • 2026 • Audio supervision & composition

Client
Kodansha
Venues
Kodansha House LA • Kodansha House NYC
Studio role
Audio supervisor · Composer · Immersive audio

Kodansha, one of Japan’s largest publishers, stages Kodansha House as a series of pop-up fan experiences. For this year's LA and NYC editions, ISIDORELU.COM served as audio supervisor and composer, writing original looping scores and rendering the immersive audio of the 10-day pop-up event. Each of the five [5] activations was built around a single Kodansha property: Attack on Titan, Blue Lock, AKIRA, Ghost in the Shell, and Witch Hat Atelier.
